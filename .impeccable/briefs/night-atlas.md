# Night Atlas — surface brief

## Scope and mode

Primary surface: the family-tree canvas (`/tree`) plus the home/dashboard (`/`).
Visitor mode: Operate.

## Audience, job, action

People documenting a family. Job: see who belongs to whom and add another person without leaving the tree. Action: select a star, choose a compass relationship, place the person.

## Constraints

Synthetic demonstration family only. In-memory API. Vite + React. Comp-led: approved composition is Open sky (`.impeccable/mocks/comp-atlas-canvas.webp`).

## Chosen direction

Night Atlas (seed `6836152f`, grounded candidate 5). Memorable moment: a selected portrait-star with four compass add actions on a navy atlas.
